Privacy statement

Last updated: September 14, 2026. This statement explains what AstraClient handles when you use the launcher.

What AstraClient does

AstraClient is a desktop launcher for Minecraft Java Edition. It stores launcher settings, local profiles, instance metadata and downloaded game files on your device so the application can work.

Authentication

If you choose Microsoft sign-in, AstraClient uses Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft Services to authenticate your account. Access tokens are used to launch the game and are stored locally by the application. AstraClient does not receive or store your Microsoft password.

Network services

The launcher may contact Mojang, Microsoft, Xbox Live, Modrinth, Minecraft loader services and image services to retrieve versions, content, account data or skins. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Data sharing

AstraClient does not sell personal data and does not operate an advertising profile. The application sends requests to the services required for the feature you use, such as sign-in, downloading content or checking a profile.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact zxer_officiel@outlook.fr.

AstraClient is independent and is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
